title: "Integrations"
description: "External platforms connected to GG3 — login and Quest verification"
---

GG3 connects to a growing set of external platforms to enable secure login, account linking, and automatic Quest verification.

## Google

- **Login method** — Allows for quick and secure login to GG3 using a Google account.
- **Data retrieval** — Automatically retrieves the username, avatar, and email address upon login.

## Steam

- **Login method** — Enables logging into GG3 through a Steam account.
- **Data retrieval** — Retrieves the username, avatar, and email.
- **Quest verification** — Communication with the Steam API allows for verification of game-related tasks, such as achievements, ownership of specific games, or hours played.

## Twitter

- **Account integration** — Allows linking a GG3 account with a Twitter account.
- **Quest verification** — Through the Twitter API, we verify tasks such as likes, retweets, follows, and replies.

## Discord

- **Account integration** — Connecting a GG3 account with Discord enables verification of activities performed on this platform.
- **Quest verification** — Checks if the user has joined a specific Discord server or obtained a particular role.

## Immutable Passport

- **Account integration** — Connecting a GG3 account to Immutable Passport allows you to verify your on-chain and off-chain activity on the Immutable and Immutable zkEVM networks.
- **Quest verification** — Checking whether a user holds specific tokens on the Immutable or Immutable zkEVM network, a specific amount of them, or specific NFTs.

## Binance (soon)

- **Binance Pay payment integration** — Enables withdrawals of earned funds directly to the user's Binance account, minimizing transaction fees.

## PayPal (soon)

- **Payment integration** — Integration with PayPal allows for the withdrawal of rewards directly to the user's PayPal account.
