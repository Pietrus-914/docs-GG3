## Overview

GGX token is deeply integrated within the GG3.ORG platform ecosystem, providing enhanced functionality, boosters, and privileges for both users participating in Quests and partners looking to increase engagement with their games and products. Here's a quick look at what GGX will be used for.

## User Benefits

### Staking & Payment Features

**1. Platform Boosters**

- **GG-Button Enhancement**
  - Ability to "freeze" the GG-Button for a specific time period.
  - Restore previous GG-Button count.
- **Raffle Quest Multipliers**
  - Tiered entry boost system.
  - Higher stakes yield greater multipliers for raffle entries.
- **XP Boost System**
  - Time-limited XP multipliers.
  - Tier-based boost levels correlating with staking amount.
- **Premium Quest Access**
  - Exclusive access to high-reward periodic Quests.
  - Special quest chains for stakers.

**2. Governance Rights\***

- Voting power proportional to staked amount.
- Participation in DAO decisions.
- Platform development voting.
- Community proposals.

## Partner Benefits

### Access & Campaign Management

**1. One-Time Payment Options**

- Customized quest packages.
- Integrated marketing campaigns.
- Platform promotion bundles.

**2. Staking-Based Benefits**

- **Campaign Creation Rights**
  - Tiered quest package access.
  - Stake-dependent campaign options.
- **Premium Support**
  - Priority platform support.
  - Enhanced analytics access.
- **DAO Participation\***
  - Voting rights on platform development.
  - Partner-specific proposals.

## Implementation Schedule

The GGX token utility features will be rolled out gradually within the gg3.org platform, ensuring stable integration and optimal user experience. The scope of the utility may change in the future and will be continuously expanded with new possibilities of using the GGX token.

This phased approach allows for:

- Systematic feature testing.
- Community feedback integration.
- Market response analysis.
- Continuous optimization.

## Additional Benefits for Token Holders

- Access to exclusive platform features.
- Priority for new feature releases.
- Enhanced reward opportunities.
- Special event participation rights.
- Community status benefits.
