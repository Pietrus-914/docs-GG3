## Types of Staking Pools

| Staking type | Lock period | APR | Token limit | Unbonding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unlimited | — | 3% | — | 7 days |
| 3 months | 3 | min 6% | 10,000,000 | — |
| 6 months | 6 | min 12% | 8,000,000 | — |
| 12 months | 12 | min 15% | 5,000,000 | — |

## 1. Unlimited Pool

- **APR**: 3%
- **Lock Period**: None
- **Unbonding Period**: 7 days
- **Key Features**:
  - Flexible entry and exit (withdrawal after 7 days from order)
  - No maximum staking limit
  - Continuous rewards accrual

## 2. Fixed-Term Pools

### Three-Month Lock Pool

- **APR**: Minimum 6%
- **Lock Period**: 3 months
- **Early Withdrawal**: Not available

### Six-Month Lock Pool

- **APR**: Minimum 12%
- **Lock Period**: 6 months
- **Early Withdrawal**: Not available

### Twelve-Month Lock Pool

- **APR**: Minimum 15%
- **Lock Period**: 12 months
- **Early Withdrawal**: Not available
