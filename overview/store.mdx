## Products and Categories

The Store offers a wide selection of products, from gift cards for popular platforms like Steam through in-game currencies such as Roblox, Riot Points as well as GTA Online dollars from Shark Cards. The range of products and categories is constantly being expanded to provide our users with the widest possible choice.

## Balance Tab

This tab on your dashboard is where you can manage your G-Bucks and make withdrawals. When you decide to withdraw funds, such as for a Steam code, a modal window will appear with information about your G-Bucks balance converted to USD, a list of available Steam top-up options, and the ability to select the amount.

## Purchases and Withdrawals

Managing purchases is intuitive — after selecting a product and quantity, you can easily convert your G-Bucks into chosen top-ups, game codes, or other available products. The purchasing process is quick and seamless, allowing you to enjoy your new purchases almost immediately.

## Receiving Codes and Services

After purchase, top-up codes or other products are almost immediately available in the **Orders** tab, where you can manage your purchases, check statuses, and collect acquired products.

## Products' Currencies

Our mission is to provide suitable products to gamers from all around the world; therefore, we enabled cards in different currencies and currently most of them are split into: **USD, EUR, PLN**. Always keep in mind your region and set currency when choosing a product from our offer.

## Complaints and Support

If you encounter problems with a code, you have the option to quickly file a complaint and contact us through the ticket system on Discord.

The GG3 Store is more than a reward exchange place — it's a space where every gamer can find something for themselves, using the rewards earned during gameplay.
