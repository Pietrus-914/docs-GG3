<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is GG3?">
    GG3 is a platform leveraging advanced AI agents to create personalized community experiences and automate interactions. Our system utilizes intelligent AI to engage users through a Quest system, where they complete various tasks ranging from social media interactions to educational activities about emerging technologies and Web3 opportunities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do you gain access to Quests?">
    To access the Quests on our platform you need to create an account. Some questlines also require connecting social accounts — such as Twitter, Steam, a Web3 wallet, Immutable wallet or Discord — reaching a certain level, or completing other Quests.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I earn G-Bucks?">
    You can earn G-Bucks in various ways. First — completing the [GG3 Tutorial](https://gg3.org/community/gg3-tutorial), winning in Raffles, Flash Drop Quests, special campaigns and G-Bucks game Quests. You may also occasionally grab G-Bucks by participating in events on our social media.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Raffles?">
    Raffles are chance-based questlines consisting of simple SubQuests. They are split into **Entries Draw** — where your chances increase the higher level you are and the more XP you have in a certain Community — and **Lucky Draw** — where all participants have equal chances.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I check if I won a Raffle?">
    You can check whether you won in a Raffle by checking the list of winners or claiming and opening the rewards Chest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I increase my chances to draw a higher reward in a Raffle?">
    If a Raffle is an **Entries Draw** type, you can increase your chances of drawing a reward from the prize pool by completing more Quests in the Community the Raffle is published in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I do with G-Bucks?">
    You can use G-Bucks to exchange for various items in our Store. Right now we offer Steam gift cards in USD, EUR and PLN as well as Riot Points and Roblox in EUR. We will add more items and currencies as we develop the platform.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a Steam account?">
    You can create a Steam account on the official [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What information is required when you sign up?">
    When you sign up we require no additional information from you. You may create an account using different login options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What login options are available?">
    You may login using Google, Steam, Discord, Twitter, crypto wallet connect and Immutable Passport (soon).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment details do I need to provide to redeem rewards?">
    You don't need to provide any payment details to redeem rewards. All you need is a GG3 Account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need to pay to participate in Quests?">
    You don't pay anything! Using our platform, participating in and completing Quests is completely free.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is XP?">
    XP are experience points you gain from completing Quests. Gaining XP increases your level and place in the Global Rankings as well as Community leaderboards.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a MainQuest?">
    A MainQuest is a primary task that consists of a number of different SubQuests (sub-tasks). When users complete all required SubQuests they complete the MainQuest and receive Chests with rewards including XP, G-Bucks and other prizes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a SubQuest?">
    A SubQuest is one of the tasks that make up a MainQuest. It involves the execution of a single action. Only XP points are awarded for completing SubQuests.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are G-Bucks?">
    G-Bucks are the currency of GG3, where 100 G-Bucks is equivalent to 1 USD. You can earn G-Bucks by completing certain Quests with the **G-Bucks** tag. You can exchange G-Bucks for various products in the GG3 Store. G-Bucks cannot be transferred between users.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Chests?">
    Chests are rewards given to users after completing MainQuests. They contain various prizes — XP, G-Bucks and other items.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the GG-Button?">
    The GG-Button is our simple daily mechanic. Every day you login you have the option to press the button, which increases your Daily Streak by 1. GG-Button streaks are required in certain Quests. More functionalities will come in the future.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Communities?">
    A Community refers to the page dedicated to a specific game, project, or influencer. It contains Quests related to the specific entity and serves as a gathering place for its community. By completing Quests in that Community you also increase your position in the Community Leaderboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I buy on GG3?">
    You can purchase various digital products, including Steam gift cards, Roblox gift cards and Riot Points. More items are coming in the future.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What game platforms are available on GG3?">
    Right now Steam and Web3 games are available. In the future we will integrate more platforms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What games are available on GG3?">
    Right now only Steam-based games and Web3 titles are available. More various games will be introduced to the platform in the future.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What to do when my code is not working?">
    Codes and items might have region restrictions. Always make sure that the currency of the product you purchase is the same as your Account Currency. If the code is not working and the currency of the products are identical, please reach out to our Support (currently available only via [Discord](https://discord.gg/paDyrkccuh) Tickets).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I list my game on GG3?">
    Yes, you can! We aim to provide as many fun and diverse games as possible to gamers. Please contact our team on [Discord](https://discord.gg/paDyrkccuh) via Tickets and we will discuss the details!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I create my own Quests?">
    If you list your game on GG3 you can decide what kind of Quests are created, as long as they are within our technical limitations. To discuss details contact our team on [Discord](https://discord.gg/paDyrkccuh) via Tickets!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I disconnect my social account and connect another one?">
    You can disconnect and connect social media accounts up to 3 times a year. If you use up your account-switching tokens you won't be able to change accounts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect my social media account to several GG3 Accounts?">
    No, you can't. Connections are per single Account basis. Moreover, Steam Quests are also tied to a single Steam account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I check my statistics?">
    Yes! We have a dedicated tab with Account [Summary](https://gg3.xyz/account?tab=summary) where you can check various statistics.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
