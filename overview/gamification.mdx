## Everything Is a Quest

In GG3, every activity transforms into a Quest. From registration, inviting friends, to daily logins — all of these are part of a bigger game where you develop your character and earn valuable rewards.

### Registration and Onboarding

Your first step in GG3 is also your first Quest. The registration process is more than just creating an account; it's an opportunity to earn your first points and unlock new possibilities.

### Inviting Friends

The referral system in GG3 is presented as a series of Quests, where each successful invitation brings rewards to both you and the invited person. Inviting friends not only helps expand the community but also gives you the chance to earn valuable rewards.

### Competitions and Events

Regular competitions and events are an important part of our platform. They not only motivate activity but also allow for competition and the chance to earn special rewards, enhancing the excitement of participating in GG3 life. Some of the tasks that appear are time-limited, and only the quickest users will have a chance to participate in them.

## Levels and Rewards

A user's account level reflects their progress on the platform. A higher level opens the door to advanced Quests and better rewards, adding a progression element known from the best RPG games.

## Daily Rewards — GG Button

You can click the GG Button every day to receive a reward. Regular logins and interactions are rewarded, encouraging daily activity and making users want to return to the platform every day.

## Reward Chests

Reward Chests are our version of loot boxes. They contain various rewards, from XP points to unique items. The moment of opening a Chest is always a surprise, adding an element of randomness and excitement.

## Leaderboard

Competition on the leaderboards is a great way to add a sporting element. Tracking your progress compared to other players motivates further gameplay and shows how your achievements stack up against the GG3 community.

At GG3, we strive to ensure that every action has deeper meaning and is part of a larger game. The goal of gamification is not only fun but also building an engaged and active community.
