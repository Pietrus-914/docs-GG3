## XP (Experience Points)

XP is the primary form of reward on GG3, earned for each completed Quest. The amount of XP you receive is clearly indicated for each task, with additional points awarded for completing entire MainQuests, where a random value drops from a Chest. XP accumulates, raising your account level on the platform, allowing you to unlock access to more advanced tasks and features.

With XP, you can also increase your chances of winning in Raffle Quests — **Entries Draw**:

- Each XP earned within one community increases the number of your **Entries** in the Raffle draw.
- Each level gained on the GG3 platform increases the number of your **Entries** by **1%** for each Raffle Quest, regardless of the community in which it occurs.

XP allows you to climb the user leaderboard. The top players in the leaderboard will have a chance to earn additional rewards.

## G-Bucks

G-Bucks is the platform's virtual currency (which is **not a cryptocurrency**). Mainly earned by completing MainQuests, G-Bucks can be exchanged for various rewards, such as vouchers or paysafecards in our Store, and in the future for withdrawals via PayPal or Binance Pay. The value of G-Bucks is fixed: **100 G-Bucks = 1 USD**.

## Reward Chests

Rewards are placed in five types of Chests, differing in rarity: **Common, Rare, Unique, Epic, Legendary**. The rarer the Chest, the more valuable the rewards it may contain. Discovering the contents of the Chests provides exciting emotions, reminiscent of the magic of draws, where you eagerly await what is inside.

## Badges

Badges in the form of graphics are awarded for completing specific MainQuests and serve as an additional achievement for the user. They can also unlock access to special Quests and communities for badge holders.

## Achievements

Achievements are awards given for cyclical activities on the platform. They consist of sets of graphics differing in color or number, related to specific actions. There are a total of **28 different achievements** available on the platform.

Categories of achievements on the GG3 platform:

- **Level** — Achievement for reaching a specific level.
- **GG-Button** — Achievements for daily clicks on the GG-Button for a certain number of days.
- **Completed Quests** — Rewards for completing a specific number of MainQuests.
- **Referral Rewards** — Achievements for inviting a certain number of friends who meet the conditions.

You can find earned Badges and Achievements in your profile.

Thanks to the variety of rewards and the exciting way of earning them, GG3 aims to offer unique experiences that motivate users to be active and learn through fun.
