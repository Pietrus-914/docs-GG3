## Quest Logic in GG3

GG3 guides the user through countless and diverse Quests. Our goal is to ensure that every element is meticulously planned. From single tasks to complex networks of Quests, everything is designed to provide valuable, educational, and exciting entertainment.

## Quest Hierarchy

Our Quests are divided into three main categories:

- **MainQuests** — Tasks that form the main storyline on the platform. Each Main Quest may consist of various stages, leading the player through complex challenges such as acquiring new knowledge, applying skills in practice, or performing specific actions.
- **SubQuests** — Side tasks related to the MainQuest. They can offer additional challenges, rewards, and exploration opportunities, helping to expand your knowledge and skills.
- **SideQuests** — Optional tasks that can be performed alongside other Quests. They offer unique rewards and challenges that can help you earn additional experience points or other benefits.

## Quest Process and Significance

In GG3, every Quest has its significance and impacts the development of your account on the platform. MainQuests are key to unlocking new opportunities and access to subsequent stages, while SubQuests and SideQuests allow you to deepen experiences and earn specific rewards.

## Quest Order Management / Quest Chains

The order of Quests is crucial to ensure a smooth transition between different tasks, therefore we organized them in **Quest Chains**: some Quests require the completion of others before starting, ensuring that you gain the appropriate skills and knowledge at the right pace.

## Quest Categories

Quests on GG3 are diverse and can be categorized in various ways, making it easier to browse and choose according to your interests:

- **Education** — Educational Quests allow you to gain new knowledge from various fields, from games to the latest technologies.
- **Web2** — Quests related to popular Web2 games. You might need to own and play a specific title, earn an achievement in the game, or spend a certain amount of time playing.
- **Web3** — Here you will find tasks related to modern blockchain solutions. After completing educational Quests, you will know how to navigate the blockchain, perform swaps, or stake tokens. Tasks in this category include owning a specific token in an EVM wallet or holding an NFT. You will also discover many Web3 game titles that can encourage you and show you the advantages of Web3 games, such as safe trading of game items.
- **Raffle** — Quests that carry the most valuable rewards. By completing SubQuests within a Raffle, you get the chance to participate in a draw for the most valuable prizes. Raffle Quests always concern a specific project (Community). We distinguish two types of Raffle:
  - **Lucky Draw** — Each participant in the draw has the same chance of winning.
  - **Entries Draw** — The chance of winning depends on the points (**Entries**) earned. Points are earned by completing Quests related to the community, and through additional boosts — each level on the GG3 platform increases your Entries by 1%.
- **Flash Drop** — Quests that, once completed, provide you with a fixed amount of G-Bucks. The prize pool is limited, so the first ones to complete are the ones rewarded.

## Why Complete Quests?

By completing Quests, you not only have fun and learn but also earn rewards that can be exchanged for various benefits, such as the platform's internal currency, digital items, paysafecards, or premium services. Every completed task is also an opportunity to advance in ranking, unlock additional Quests, and participate in special Quests.
